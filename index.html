Rules & privacy

Scoring: a correct guess scores 100 points with one clue, 75 with two, 50 with three, and 25 with four. A wrong guess costs 10 points but you can try again. Scores cannot fall below zero.

Trap Card: use it once per game to remove one incorrect answer. It does not reveal the player.

Privacy: this prototype does not send your answers or scores to a server. Your best scores and game count are stored locally in your browser. The game uses no analytics or advertising trackers.

Sharing: the challenge button opens your device's share menu where available, or copies a message to your clipboard. Friends play the same daily set on the same date.
