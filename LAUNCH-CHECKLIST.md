# Transfer Trap — pre-launch checklist

## Product
- [ ] Ask 5–10 football fans to play without coaching.
- [ ] Confirm clue difficulty feels fair and answers are unambiguous.
- [ ] Verify the Trap Card works once per game and cannot be reused.
- [ ] Verify scores for 1, 2, 3 and 4 clues.
- [ ] Verify Daily Trap uses the same five questions for all users on the same UTC date.
- [ ] Test the share button on iPhone, Android and desktop.
- [ ] Test narrow mobile screens and keyboard-only navigation.

## Editorial / legal
- [ ] Fact-check all questions against reliable sources.
- [ ] Keep a source note for each clue in the maintained question bank.
- [ ] Search UK IPO trade mark records and domain availability for the final name.
- [ ] Do not use club crests, player photos, copied scripts, or another game's distinctive branding without permission/licensing.
- [ ] Review privacy, cookies and consumer terms before adding third-party analytics or accounts.

## Growth test
- [ ] Share with 10 relevant group chats/communities where promotion is allowed.
- [ ] Ask testers to share only if they genuinely enjoyed it.
- [ ] Record: invite-to-play rate, completed games, repeat play within 7 days, and number of new players per sharer.
- [ ] Decide whether to iterate, pivot the mechanic, or stop before spending on ads.


## Scope / difficulty / era filters (spec v0.2)
- [ ] Setup defaults to all leagues worldwide + hard + all eras.
- [ ] Geography, difficulty and era can be changed independently.
- [ ] Player eligibility is based on verified league appearances, not nationality.
- [ ] Only source-reviewed, published questions enter public games.
- [ ] Daily challenge set is deterministic for UTC date and exact filter combination.
- [ ] Insufficient eligible questions produces a clear message; filters are never silently broadened.
- [ ] Share link/result records the scope, difficulty, era and daily date.
- [ ] Worldwide and all-era coverage claims match actual verified data coverage.
